01 — THE CONCEPT

Separate the light
from the detail.

Homomorphic filtering reduces uneven illumination and improves contrast by separating slowly changing illumination from fine reflectance details in the frequency domain.

f(x,y) = i(x,y) × r(x,y)

Three ways to shape
the frequency response.

Common high-pass transfer functions used in homomorphic filtering.

01 / CLASSIC

Ideal high-pass

02 / SMOOTH

Gaussian

05 — PRACTICAL CONSIDERATIONS

Powerful, with
some trade-offs.

Parameter sensitivity — Poorly tuned values may look unnatural.

Noise amplification — High-frequency gain can amplify sensor noise.

Not a relighting model — Lost shadow or clipped highlight detail cannot be recovered.

Color considerations — The LAB luminance channel is filtered to preserve color.
